# School and Community Action Framework

## School AQI Operating Protocols
Schools have a duty of care to protect children whose lung systems are especially vulnerable:
- **AQI < 100 (Good / Satisfactory):** Normal outdoor sports, physical education, and morning assemblies permitted.
- **AQI 101–200 (Moderate):** Outdoor activities permitted; children with diagnosed respiratory conditions should be offered indoor alternatives.
- **AQI 201–300 (Poor):** Suspend rigorous endurance sports (e.g., long-distance running, cross-country, soccer matches). Hold morning assembly indoors or over PA systems.
- **AQI > 300 (Very Poor / Severe):** Mandatory indoor recess and sports cancellation. Classrooms should remain sealed with air purifiers or DIY Corsi-Rosenthal boxes running on medium speed. Implement strict anti-idling policies for parents and buses around school gates.

## Resident Welfare Association (RWA) & Neighborhood Action
- **Car-Free Fridays:** Encourage residents to use bicycles, carpooling, or walking for short neighborhood errands.
- **Zero Open Burning Vigilance:** Strict zero-tolerance for burning dry leaves, municipal solid waste, or biomass. Set up community compost pits for dry garden waste.
- **Security & Staff Well-being:** Provide certified N95 masks and clean electric heating appliances (avoid open fire braziers/sigris) for night guards and outdoor sanitation workers.
- **Shared Purified Zones:** Designate the community clubhouse or library as a shared clean-air sanctuary with HEPA/C-R filtration during severe winter smog episodes.
