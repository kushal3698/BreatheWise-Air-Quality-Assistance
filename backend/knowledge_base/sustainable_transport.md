# Sustainable Urban Mobility & Low-Emission Commute

## Emission Factors & Air Quality Impact
Internal combustion engine vehicles are responsible for a large share of urban particulate matter and nitrogen oxides:
- **Private Petrol Car:** ~0.170 kg CO2 per vehicle-km, plus tailpipe PM2.5, carbon monoxide, and unburned hydrocarbons.
- **Diesel SUV/Car:** ~0.171 kg CO2/km, but produces up to 10–20x higher PM2.5 and NOx than petrol per vehicle.
- **Electric Vehicle (EV):** Zero tailpipe emissions. Grid lifecycle emissions: ~0.045 kg CO2/km.
- **City Bus:** ~0.022 kg CO2 per passenger-km (assuming average occupancy).
- **Metro Rail:** ~0.014 kg CO2 per passenger-km. Highly efficient and isolated from ground-level road congestion.

## Actionable Commute Hacks
1. **Trip Chaining:** Batching errands into a single trip prevents cold starts (where catalytic converters operate below optimal temperature and emit 3x more pollutants).
2. **Shift to Metro / Electric Bus:** Avoids sitting in idling traffic where in-cabin air quality deteriorates rapidly.
3. **Turn Off Engine at Signals:** Idling for more than 15 seconds consumes more fuel and generates more toxic localized emissions than restarting the engine.
4. **School Transport Pooling:** Replacing individual private car drop-offs with a shared school bus or carpool eliminates dozens of vehicle trips right at school perimeters.
