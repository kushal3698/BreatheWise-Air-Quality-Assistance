# Masks, Respirators, and Personal Protection Guide

## Filtration Efficacy Comparison
- **Cloth & Handkerchiefs:** Less than 15–25% filtration efficiency for 0.3–2.5 micron particles. Provides a false sense of security; does not prevent alveolar penetration.
- **Surgical 3-ply Masks:** Offer ~30–45% real-world particulate protection due to massive perimeter leakage around nose and cheeks.
- **Certified Respirators (N95 / FFP2 / KN95):** Filter at least 95% of fine particulate matter (0.3 micron aerodynamic diameter). Must possess an airtight seal against the face.

## Fit, Seal & Best Practices
- **Pinch the metal nose clip:** Mold the bridge firmly across the contour of the nose to eliminate gap leakage.
- **Check for leaks:** Inhale sharply; the respirator should pull slightly inward. If air leaks near eyes or jaw, readjust straps.
- **Facial hair impact:** Even a short stubble prevents a complete gasket seal, reducing protection by up to 50%.
- **Valved respirators:** Exhalation valves reduce humidity inside the mask and ease breathing, but do not filter exhaled breath. Useful for healthy commuters in polluted outdoor transit.

## Reuse & Decontamination
- Do not wash N95 respirators with water, alcohol, or soap; liquid compromises the electrostatic charge of meltblown fibers.
- Store used respirators in a clean, breathable paper bag for 48–72 hours between uses in dry conditions.
- Replace the mask if the headband loses elasticity, breathing resistance noticeably increases, or the mask becomes soiled or crushed.
